Quiet, stoic, but blunt when she does speak, with the occasional dry wit.

Violet holds herself to high standards, meaning she just has to excel at everything she does as is her perceived obligation to her family. She sometimes fears she is on the path to being a disappointment compared to her childhood, and that she will fail to earn her parents' pride when they inevitably reunite.

Coming from a crime family, her morals are not as righteous as befitting a hero, resorting to violence more often than she should, much to Haven's (and everyone else's) chagrin. She doesn't hold human life as precious as her partner. She tends to look down on others, falling prey to arrogance at times. However, if she respects someone, or even cares about them, her loyalty is endless.

BACKSTORY

After her criminal family is arrested during a raid ,

when she is but a wee child, Violet throws herself into becoming a highly ranked hero under the awkward guidance of her parents' right-hand man, who escapes capture with her but is permanently injured in the hip during the escape. He acts as her 'uncle' in public.

Violet is free to rise through the hero standings

As her family used one of her cousins to pose as her during the raid, determined to gain certain privileges so she may one day free her family from their (just?) imprisonment. Against her better judgement however, she accepts Haven's offer to team up as 'partners in heroics', which will undoubtedly slow her down, especially when Haven doesn't have any powers.

In an interview with a higher-up, she allows Haven to take up the title

If she participates and performs well in an upcoming tournament event; her appearance as an underdog must be impressive enough so admitting her into the industry will be treated as a magnanimous decision and boost their public status.

Haven is slightly awed by Violet while fighting her in the tournament and pesters her into becoming 'partners in heroics'.
