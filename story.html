STORY

SUMMARY

A long time ago, humans evolved, and now majority of this world’s population are born with powers.

First, villains had emerged from the fray, using their powers for evil, but heroes had countered them soon after, developing into a recognized industry for the purpose of making the world a better place. Programs were created to train potential heroes, open to anyone willing to join, or so they say.

f

f

Haven is one such individual.

She is passionate about helping others, partially because of her past, but mainly because it is simply her nature. Her lifelong goal is to be the highest-ranked hero, so she can help the most people possible.

However, because of her history and lack of powers, she is rejected from the industry. In an interview with a certain higher-up, she will allow Haven into the program she runs if Haven participates and performs well in an upcoming tournament event for trainees from different hero program; her appearance as an underdog must be impressive enough so admitting her into the program will be treated as a magnanimous decision and boost their public status.

During the tournament, Haven fights in the quarterfinals against

Violet, a stoic trainee whose few words are as sharp as her sword

and would be annoyingly haughty if she didn’t have the prowess to support her pride, and

Lumen, the the industry’s star trainee

who is widely judged to be a steep contender for the number one title. She loses to him, but her opponents and even other participants vouch for her to join the program, so the higher-up relents, and allows Haven to join her hero training program.
